# Speed Switch Hero Art Bible

## Palette

- Clean base: `#F8F7F3`
- Primary text / authority: `#1A1A1A`
- Accent / CTA gold: `#CFA64A`
- Muted body text: soft gray around `#777B82`
- Surfaces: warm white or translucent white for elevated panels
- Dividers: black at roughly 10% opacity
- Shadows: warm, soft, low-opacity gold or brown shadows

The palette should feel warm, premium, restrained, and clean. Gold is an accent, not a full-page theme.

## Typography Rules

- Headings use `Space Grotesk`.
- Body text and UI use `Manrope`.
- Major headings should be bold, uppercase, large, dark, and tightly stacked.
- Body text should be medium gray, readable, and calm.
- Avoid decorative fonts, script fonts, or overly thin weights.
- Future section headings can use `Space Grotesk`, but they should scale down from the hero. The hero remains the largest type moment on the page.

## Spacing Rules

- Use generous top-level spacing.
- Keep layouts centered and balanced.
- Let large visual sections breathe with wide margins.
- Keep UI controls padded, but not oversized.
- Maintain a compact vertical rhythm inside forms, cards, and controls.
- Avoid cramped feature grids or dense marketing clutter.
- Keep one strong idea per section.

## Layout Principles

- Use a centered premium composition.
- Keep strong horizontal alignment.
- Make hierarchy obvious: headline first, support copy second, action/control third, image asset last.
- Avoid asymmetric redesigns unless the image composition requires it.
- Use full-width bands or clean unframed sections.
- Avoid nested cards.
- Future sections should stay spacious, direct, image-led, and easy to scan.

## Image Treatment

- Use real product or vehicle imagery as the visual anchor.
- Keep vehicles large and prominent.
- Do not over-darken images.
- Use subtle gradients or vignettes only for readability and depth.
- Preserve clean studio-like backgrounds.
- Shadows should feel natural and grounded.
- Avoid stock-photo clutter, heavy blur, busy lifestyle backgrounds, and decorative image effects.

## Motion Rules

- Motion should be subtle and premium.
- Buttons may lift slightly on hover.
- Hover transitions should be fast and smooth, around `180ms`.
- Use easing similar to `cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.8, 0.2, 1)`.
- Avoid bouncy, playful, or excessive animation.
- Avoid distracting entrance animations unless they are very restrained.

## Button Style

- Primary buttons use `#CFA64A`.
- Button text uses `#1A1A1A`, not white.
- Radius should be modest, around `8px`.
- Buttons should be solid, rectangular, and premium.
- Use subtle warm shadows.
- Include simple icon support where useful, such as arrow or search icons.
- Hover treatment: slight upward movement, mild shadow increase, and subtle saturation.

## Nav Style

- Header is clean and spacious.
- Logo sits left.
- Main nav is centered on desktop.
- Actions sit right.
- Nav links are dark, medium-bold, and minimal.
- Active state uses a small gold dot below the link.
- Profile icon is outlined and circular.
- Avoid heavy nav backgrounds or boxed nav containers.

## Mobile Adaptation Rules

- Hide desktop nav links on mobile.
- Keep logo and primary CTA visible.
- Stack booking/search fields vertically.
- Preserve headline impact, but prevent text overflow.
- Keep the vehicle image as the lower visual anchor.
- Buttons should become full-width when inside stacked panels.
- Preserve readable spacing instead of squeezing the desktop layout onto mobile.

## Future Sections Should Feel Like

- Premium automotive rental, not generic SaaS.
- Clean, warm, confident, and image-led.
- Large vehicle visuals should carry the page.
- Typography should be restrained, bold, and direct.
- UI chrome should stay minimal.
- Copy should be short and specific.
- Gold should be used only for actions, highlights, active states, and premium accents.
- Avoid busy feature grids, over-rounded cards, purple or blue gradients, decorative blobs, and generic landing-page patterns.
