# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Two equal segments arriving at the same surface:

- **Dubai visitors / tourists**: planning or mid-trip, high intent to book fast, often price-anchoring against taxis or Uber. They need immediate trust (photos, reviews, familiar brands) and a frictionless path to WhatsApp.
- **Dubai residents**: comparison-shopping the local market, familiar with rental norms, looking for proof of fair pricing, deposit reliability, and team reputation. They read the reviews.

Both groups open on mobile first. Both convert via WhatsApp. Neither wants to fill out a form.

## Product Purpose

Speed Switch is a Dubai-based luxury car rental service. The site's job is to make a stranger trust the business in under 30 seconds and give them one clear action: start a WhatsApp conversation.

Success = a new WhatsApp message initiated from any CTA on the page.

## Brand Personality

Confident. Warm. Grounded.

Not flashy, not corporate. Speed Switch is the well-regarded local expert — they know the city, they know the cars, they treat you like a person. The tone is assured without being boastful. The design is premium without being cold or unapproachable.

Three-word anchor: **assured, hospitable, precise**.

## References

Two inspiration images provided:
1. A "RENTAL" landing page: white background, saturated amber/golden yellow accent, bold uppercase display headings, car floating on geometric color fill, generous whitespace, clean search widget.
2. "RoadTripRent": similar amber + white palette, bold impactful headings, large car photography, structured section rhythm, approachable premium energy.

What to extract: **saturated amber-yellow accent** (more vibrant than a muted ochre gold), **white-dominant base** (not warm cream), **uppercase bold headings**, **cars as hero visual**, **confident but not cold**.

## Anti-references

- Budget/bargain rental sites: coupon-heavy, cluttered, low-trust layouts
- Generic SaaS / startup aesthetic: cream backgrounds, metric-card heroes, eyebrow-above-every-section scaffolding
- Flashy streetwear / car influencer aesthetic: neon, dark aggressive, TikTok energy
- Overly corporate: cold blues, stock photography, no personality
- Ultra-exclusive luxury (Bentley.com tier): untouchably remote, not warm or hospitable

## Design Principles

1. **Show, don't tell**: real car photos, real review quotes, real team names — never stock-photo generic
2. **One action per screen**: every section funnels toward the same destination (WhatsApp booking), never competing CTAs
3. **Earned confidence**: premiumness is communicated through precision (spacing, typography, color consistency) not through loudness
4. **The car is the hero**: layout decisions defer to the photography; the car always reads first
5. **Local and human**: Dubai delivery, Ahmad and the team, Arabic-friendly layout considerations — specificity builds trust

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Best-effort baseline: readable contrast for body text, keyboard-navigable CTAs, meaningful alt text on car images. No strict WCAG level required.
